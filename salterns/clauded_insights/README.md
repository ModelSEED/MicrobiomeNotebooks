## TL;DR — the three findings that matter

1. **Figure 7 disagrees with the text.** Every R² in the modelling Results/Discussion
   (0.978, 0.966, 0.974, 0.862, 0.852, 0.798) is from a pre-DNA-weighting analysis and
   matches **nothing** in the current data; the submitted figure shows different values
   again (0.89 / 0.30 / 0.58 / 0.19). → `numbers_to_reconcile.md`.
2. **The strong correlations are partly a normalisation artefact.** Dividing both axes by
   DNA density inflates them (spurious correlation of ratios). On a consistent raw
   areal-emission basis, MePn model-vs-measured falls from R² = 0.89 → **0.37** (ρ = 0.60,
   p = 0.21, n = 6 — not significant).
3. **"MePn ≫ betaine" is overstated.** With all 12 cores on raw units, betaine-consumer
   abundance tracks methane (ρ = 0.70, p = 0.011) about as well as MePn-consumer abundance
   (ρ = 0.73, p = 0.007) — **both significant**. MePn is still the better-supported route
   (more variance explained, plus the independent culturing and substrate-addition
   evidence), but the separation is far narrower than stated. The near-zero betaine result
   was an artefact of the n = 6 unrestored subset.

The core conclusion (MePn as a leading methane source in the unrestored salterns) still
stands — it rests on Figure 4 (substrate additions) and Figures 5–6 (Halomonas culturing),
which do **not** depend on the n = 6 modelling.

## What was corrected in Figure 7 (detail)

- **Trendline rendering.** The submitted code evaluated `y = m·x + b` only at the 6 data
  points and joined them in raw-x order on log axes → the broken vertical-then-flat curve.
  Fixed by evaluating the *same* linear fit on a dense x-grid (smooth on log axes).
- **Kept** the linear fitted equation (intuitive units) and log axes (all points in one
  frame) — per request.
- **Units made consistent** so a 1:1 model-validation line is meaningful (the submitted
  panel a divided the measured axis by DNA while the modelled axis scaled with biomass≈DNA).
- **Honest statistics**: n, Pearson R², and Spearman ρ with its exact p on every series.
- **All 12 cores** in panel b (was 6 unrestored), coloured by restoration — recovers
  significance and shows the restoration gradient.

## Still-open items flagged for the authors (not changed here)

- **Phosphate ratio** (0.07) is a tuned parameter — lead with the parameter-free
  (unconstrained) fit, and/or tie it to the measured Olsen-P per core.
- **No archaea in the abundance-weighted MAG set** (280 Bacteria, 0 archaea): the
  betaine→TMA→methane route is closed with an empirical 0.06 × TMA scalar, not a modelled
  methanogen. State explicitly.
- **Figure 8 network**: n = 12 over ~140 taxa (p ≫ n) — report bootstrap edge-stability
  rather than a single fit.

## ⚠ Caveat on the voice-model output

`manuscript_corrected.andrew_voice.md` is produced by the local `andrew-voice` LoRA, which
paraphrases and can occasionally introduce a stray clause. To protect the science the
driver (a) passes headings, captions, References, Data Availability and Acknowledgments
through **verbatim**, and (b) applies a **citation guard** — if a rewritten paragraph drops
or invents any numeric citation, the original paragraph is kept. Even so, treat it as a
**stylistic draft** and reconcile it against `manuscript_corrected.md` before use.
